Featured Destination

Lake Malawi

An inland freshwater sea teeming with hundreds of species of colorful cichlid fish, bordered by golden sandy beaches and offering premium kayaking, snorkeling, and relaxation.

Top Attractions

Liwonde National Park

Malawi's premier wildlife destination featuring river boat safaris, elephants, hippos, and a flourishing black rhino sanctuary along the majestic Shire River.

Mount Mulanje

The magnificent island in the sky. Massive granite mountains rising above lush tea estates, perfect for multi-day trekking and hiking adventures.

Essential Travel Tips

  • Best Time to Visit: May to October during the cool, dry winter season when wildlife viewing is optimal.
  • Currency: Malawian Kwacha (MWK). ATMs are readily available in major urban centers.
  • Entry Requirements: Check e-visa portals before traveling; many nationalities get visa-free entry or visas on arrival.
